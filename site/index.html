Where the numbers come from

Listings and reviews come from Amazon Reviews '23 (McAuley Lab, UC San Diego), with reviews through September 2023, for four categories: Pet Supplies, Beauty & Personal Care, Sports & Outdoors, and Home & Kitchen.

  • Which listings. Listings priced $10–$100 with 100+ ratings make up the niche board. The product search covers the ones with 1,000+ ratings, so a lookup compares you with sellers who have proven demand.
  • Complaint and praise rates are counted over every review of a listing, not a sample. Complaint rates use 1–2 star reviews and praise rates use 4–5 star reviews. They come from keyword rules, so read the quotes before trusting a rate.
  • Quotes are the most helpful verified reviews of each listing: two low, one middle and two high. They are not a random sample.
  • Trend compares reviews written in 2021 with 2022, the last two full years, relative to the whole category. The dataset has fewer 2022 reviews than 2021 in every category, a collection effect rather than a real slump, so a raw drop would make nearly everything look like it was fading. Reviews in 2023 run only through September.
  • Prices are from when the data was collected (2023) and there are no supplier prices, so cost and shipping estimates are labeled as estimates.

What it can't tell you

  • Anything after September 2023. Check TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends and Amazon's current best sellers before you spend money.
  • How your store will sell. Amazon buyers get fast shipping and easy returns, while your buyers come from ads and wait longer. Treat demand here as proof that people want the thing, not as a forecast.
  • Whether a product looks good on video. Claude's verdict gives a judgment on that, labeled as one.

Scorecard rules

  • Demand is green when the matched listings hold 30,000+ ratings in total, and amber at 8,000+.
  • Trend is green when reviews grew 10% or more faster than the category from 2021 to 2022, and red when they fell 15% or more behind it.
  • Competition is red when one seller holds half the matched ratings or the niche's top three hold half, and amber at 30%.
  • Margin is green at 3x markup over cost plus shipping with $15+ profit before ads, and amber at 2x or $8+.
  • Shipping is amber over 2 lb, over 18 inches or for fragile products, and red over 5 lb or 30 inches.
  • Returns is green when under 8% of reviews are 1–2 stars, and red at 13% or more.
  • Rules and ad policy is red when 40%+ of matches are ingestible, topical, pesticides or weapons, and amber for batteries, plug-ins, aerosols or kids' products.
