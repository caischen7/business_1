_Product Looker analysis, Amazon Reviews '23 (listings and reviews through September 2023). Written October 2026._

Picked by screening all 444,673 listings for: 2,000+ ratings, a $20–$60 price, reviews growing at least 30% faster than their category from 2021 to 2022, 2 lb or lighter, 18 in or shorter, and no ingestible, topical, pesticide, weapon, battery, plug-in, kids' or sized flags. Twenty-two listings passed. Cat window perches were the best product *type* among them: real growth across many sellers, no electronics, an open field and an obvious video.

## 6. Risks

1. **A falling perch is a safety incident, not just a refund.** The top low-star reviews describe falls, a trapped paw and broken glass. As the seller, you own that. Sell only a supported design, get product liability insurance before scaling, and never sell the wire-loop hammock. Confidence: **high**.
2. **Margin is thinner than it looks.** The product is bulky, about 3 lb, and in 2023 buyers could get a hammock for $16 on Amazon. If a supported perch costs you more than $12 landed, or shipping tops $10, the $15 profit floor goes. Confidence: **medium**, because the costs are assumptions.
3. **Size surprises.** Buyers complain it's wider or larger than pictured and doesn't fit their window. Slower delivery makes returns more painful for a dropshipper than for Amazon. Confidence: **medium**.
4. **The data is from 2023.** Temu and TikTok Shop may now sell perches for under $15, and the field may be crowded. Confidence: **unknown until checked**.

## 7. Opportunities

- **Sell safety as the product.** Use legs or a brace that rest on the sill, suction cups only as a backup, no exposed wire loops, and rubber bumpers on the glass. Each of these answers a specific complaint above, and supported perches already halve the low-star share.
- **Put the measurements first.** Show a "fits windows from X to Y" graphic and a fit checker on the product page to head off the size returns.
- **Bundle to raise order value:** a washable spare cover, or two perches for multi-cat homes (several reviewers bought two).

## 8. Recommended tests (cheapest first)

1. **Check 2026 prices (free, one hour).** Search "cat window perch" in TikTok Creative Center, Amazon's current best sellers, AliExpress or CJ (filter for supported, legged or sill models), and Temu. Kill the idea if supported models land above $14 with shipping, or if TikTok Shop sells them under $20.
2. **Order three supplier samples ($60–$100).** Load-test each with 20 lb for 48 hours on a real window, measure the installed width, and check for exposed wires. Keep only one that passes. Film the test as ad footage.
3. **Landing page and a $100 ad test.** One product, $39.99, with the fit checker. Set the kill rule before launch: stop if the cost per purchase is over $18 after $100 of spend.

## 9. Verdict

**Change it, then test it.** People clearly want cat window perches, and demand was rising through 2023. But the common $16 suction-cup hammock produces falls, injury reports and returns, which is the wrong risk for a new store to own. Sell a supported, sill-braced perch at about $39.99, where reviews show half the share of unhappy buyers.

**Confidence: medium.** Demand and the safety pattern are well supported. Supplier cost, 2026 competition and margin are assumptions.

**Cheapest next test:** order three supported-perch samples and load-test them on a window. If one passes and lands under $14, run a $100 ad test.
